Why Jangadeiro?

A jangadeiro navigates by understanding wind, forces, balance, direction and motion. We bring the same physics-first thinking to flight simulation.

Jangadeiro is an independent simulation-engineering concept focused on clear models for complex vehicles. The objective is simple: give engineering teams a virtual aircraft they can use to design, test and understand the real one.
